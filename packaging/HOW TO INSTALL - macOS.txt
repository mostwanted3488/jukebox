JUKEBOX - by BUY OR BE A LOSER (@buyorbealoser)
================================================

HOW TO INSTALL (macOS)

1. Copy the plugin(s) into your plugin folders
   - JUKEBOX.vst3      ->  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/
                           (FL Studio, Ableton, Bitwig, Studio One, Reaper...)
   - JUKEBOX.component ->  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/
                           (Logic Pro, GarageBand)

   Tip: in Finder press Cmd + Shift + G and paste the folder path.
   "~" means your user folder (the "Library" folder is hidden by default).

2. Allow the plugin to load (needed once)
   The plugin is not notarised by Apple, so macOS blocks it until you run
   this command. Open the Terminal app and paste:

   xattr -cr ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/JUKEBOX.vst3 ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/JUKEBOX.component

   then press Enter.

3. Rescan your plugins
   - FL Studio: Options > Manage plugins > Find installed plugins,
     then add JUKEBOX as a generator (Instrument > BUY OR BE A LOSER > JUKEBOX).
   - Logic: restart Logic, it validates the AU automatically.

Works on Apple Silicon (M1/M2/M3/M4) and Intel Macs, macOS 10.13 or newer.

HOW TO USE
- Drag a disc into the jukebox (or click it). Click the jukebox to eject.
- Play from your piano roll / MIDI keyboard.
- Lever (or L key): redstone pulse gate synced to your project tempo.
